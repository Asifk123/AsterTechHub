## 🛡️ Terms of Agreement & Billing Guidelines

### 💳 Payment Milestones (For Fixed Projects)
- **Advance Deposit:** 30% upon signing the agreement (to initiate UI/UX wireframing).
- **UI/UX Approval:** 20% upon completion and approval of visual designs.
- **Development Milestone:** 30% upon functional demo (staging environment).
- **Launch & Handover:** 20% upon deployment to production and credentials delivery.

### 📝 Monthly Retainer Billing (For Digital Marketing)
- Monthly retainers are invoiced on the **1st of every month** and are payable within **5 business days**.
- Ad budgets (Google/Meta Ads) are directly funded by the client and are separate from Aster Tech Hub's management retainer fees.

### ⏱️ Timeline & Support SLAs
- All target timelines are subject to timely client feedback and assets delivery (brand assets, copywriting material).
- **Support:** Standard fixed packages include 30 days of post-launch hypercare support covering critical bug fixes and server adjustments.
